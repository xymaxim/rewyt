# Usage disclaimer

## Not affiliated with YouTube

Rewyt isn't made by YouTube, and it has no official connection to them.

## About YouTube's rules

The app unfortunately violates YouTube’s [Terms of
Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). YouTube only allows access to its
content through its own apps or website. The app plays live streams a different
way. It doesn't download or save anything, but it still goes against that rule.

Use it at your own risk. If YouTube notices, you might get rate-limited or
temporarily blocked.

## Use it responsibly

Support the people you watch by subscribing to their channel and liking or
commenting on YouTube. If you share a clip or screenshot, give credit to the
original video and their channel. The app was built for personal use, to watch
streams and keep favorite moments, not to redistribute them elsewhere.
